\chapter*{About this manual}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this manual}
This manual documents what \fwt{} adds to the \wtwof{} toolbox: installation of the whole
package, the coupling to Higher-Order Spectral (HOS) wave solvers, the \app{waveIsoFoam}
solver, ocean currents, floating bodies with FloatStepper, and moorings with MoorDyn.

Everything that \fwt{} inherits unchanged from \wtwof{} -- the relaxation zone technique,
the algebraic wave theories, the pre- and post-processing utilities and their input files --
is described in the \emph{waves2Foam Manual} by N.~G.~Jacobsen~\cite{jacobsen2017manual}
and is not repeated here. Where a chapter relies on such material, it gives the section of
that manual to read.

The manual is a draft. Please report errors and unclear passages on the issue tracker of
the \fwt{} repository.

Claude Code, with Anthropic's Claude Opus models, was used in writing this manual and in
developing parts of \fwt{} (the installation script, the MoorDyn restraints for
\kw{sixDoFRigidBodyMotion}, bug fixes and tutorial fixes). All text and code produced with the
tool have been reviewed by the author, who takes full responsibility for them.
